Encryption

Encryption is the process of converting readable data (plaintext) into an unreadable format (ciphertext) so only authorized people can read it. Think of it like, when you text someone through messages, there is a background process that makes sure that no one else except you and the other person can read the message, not even the app provider company (whatsapp, apple, google, etc.) can read your messages.

Caesar Cipher encryption demo
Plain (Type here):
Shift (Use slider): 3
Encrypted (Result) KHOOR ZRUOG
Modern encryption uses AES-256 — which would take billions of years to crack by brute force by a hacker who would try to hack in
HTTPS uses TLS encryption to protect everything that you send online like messages or photos
End-to-end encryption means that only you and the recipient can read your messages, not even app provider can see your messages
Caesar Cipher is now an obselete form of encryption in this modern tech world

03
Firewalls

A firewall is a security system that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic based on predefined rules kind of like a bouncer for your network. This is a very necessary system required on any device because it serves a great purpose, Ex: Inspects data, Blocks threats,etc.

There are two types of firewalls: Network firewalls (hardware) in your home or router, and Host-based firewalls (software), a program built directly into your computer or phone
Next-gen firewalls (NGFW) can inspect the actual content of packets, not just ports
